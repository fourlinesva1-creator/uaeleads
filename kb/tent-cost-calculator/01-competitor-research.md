# 01 — Competitor Research

> Researched 2026-10-05 (English and Arabic searches).
> **Headline: no interactive tent cost calculator exists for the UAE, in either language.**

---

| Who | Type | Offers | Misses |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Tents Galaxy](https://www.tentsgalaxy.com/how-much-does-it-cost-to-rent-a-majlis-tent) (UAE) | Static price article | Ranges by size (e.g. majlis 20–50 guests AED 3,000–12,000 per event), an included/excluded list, 8 FAQs | No calculator, no Arabic, ranges too wide to plan with, phone/email as the only next step |
| [Shades Galaxy](https://www.shadesgalaxy.com/how-much-does-a-storage-tent-rental-cost) (UAE) | Static price article | Storage tent cost ranges | Same as above |

| [Reservety](https://reservety.com/tools/event-rental-cost-estimator.html) (US) | Interactive estimator | Event type + guest count → list of items with low/high estimates; other sites can embed it | US averages in USD, no length of hire, no AC, no add-on options, no quote request |
| [Omni Calculator](https://omnicalculator.com/everyday-life/tent-size) | Size calculator | Seating style, guests, bars, buffets, waiter stations → m² / sq ft | **No price.** No layout drawing |

| [Marquee Tech](https://marqueetech.co.uk/features/quote-building) (UK) | Software for rental companies | Quote builder, [floor planner drawn to scale](https://www.marqueetech.co.uk/free-tools-for-tent-companies/free-floor-planner-tool) | Built for rental companies, not customers |
| [AddToEvent](https://www.addtoevent.co.uk/marquee2hire) (UK) | Quote marketplace | 2–3 minute request form, add-ons (flooring, lighting, heating, bar) | Shows no price. Collects leads for many suppliers |

Arabic search ("حاسبة تكلفة تأجير خيمة رمضان الإمارات") returned only news articles and venue
iftar menus. **There is no Arabic competitor at all.**

Useful facts for our written content: Ramadan tents run AED 15,000 to 200,000 in the press,
and putting up a tent without a Civil Defence permit carries a **AED 10,000 fine**
([Emarat Al Youm](https://www.emaratalyoum.com/local-section/other/2018-05-13-1.1098461)).

---

## The gap we fill

Nobody combines these in one tool:

1. **Guests → tent size → price** (Omni does size only; Reservety does price only).
2. **UAE-specific costs**: AC sizing, Civil Defence and municipality permits, emirate-based delivery, the Ramadan peak season.
3. **Included and not included** shown for each line, not as a general note at the bottom.
4. **Two pricing models**: events (per day) and long-term (per m² per month).
5. **Full Arabic with right-to-left layout.**
6. **A next step for the visitor**: WhatsApp with the estimate filled in, a printable quote, a shareable link.

## Space per guest (for the size suggester)

Converted from Omni and Giggster figures (1 m² ≈ 10.76 sq ft). The majlis figure is our own
assumption and **needs the ops team to confirm it**.

| Seating style | m² per guest |
|---|---|
| Standing / reception | 0.6–0.75 |
| Theatre / prayer rows | 0.75–0.9 |
| Banquet (round tables) | 1.0–1.2 |
| Banquet + dance floor/stage | 1.4–1.9 |
| **Majlis (sofas or floor seating)** | **3–4** *(based on the 8×10 job for 20 guests; was 1.5–2 — confirm with ops)* |

Fixed additions: bar ≈ 9 m² each, buffet ≈ 9 m² each, stage ≈ 18 m². Add 15–20% for walkways.
Pole tents lose 10–15% of usable space to the centre poles. Frame tents lose none.
