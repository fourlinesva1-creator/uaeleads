What is the difference between an Iftar tent, a Suhoor tent, and a general Ramadan Majlis?
The primary difference lies in their functionality and atmosphere:

Iftar Tent: Designed for the breaking of the fast. It requires efficient flow for buffet service, ample seating, and bright, welcoming lighting. The focus is on accommodating a large number of guests comfortably for a meal.
Suhoor Tent: Created for the pre-dawn meal. The atmosphere is more relaxed, intimate, and lounge-like. It features softer, moodier lighting, deeper seating for lounging, and often areas designated for shisha (with proper ventilation).
General Ramadan Majlis: A versatile space used throughout the Holy Month for hosting guests, socializing, and conducting business. It balances comfort with elegance and often features a blend of traditional and modern design elements.

How is the required AC tonnage calculated for a tent?
AC tonnage is not a guess; it's a calculation based on several factors. Our experts use a formula that considers:

Tent Dimensions: Length, width, and height (volume of the space).
Occupancy: The number of people expected (each person generates body heat).
Heat Sources: Buffet stations, lighting, and any electrical equipment inside the tent.
Insulation & Sun Exposure: The amount of direct sunlight the tent fabric will receive.
Desired Temperature: The target indoor temperature (typically a comfortable 20-22°C for Iftar/Suhoor).
For example, a small tent (e.g., 10m x 10m x 3m) might need 5-7 tons of cooling, while a large structure (e.g., 30m x 50m x 6m) could require 50-70 tons or more. We never undersize our AC units. Our experience ensures we provide slightly more capacity than needed to guarantee peak performance, even on the most humid days.
